1. Who operates TruMotif Browser Guard

TruMotif Browser Guard is provided by ParadigmIT Cybersecurity as part of the TruMotif browser security and AI-governance platform.

In an enterprise deployment, the customer's organization generally determines how the extension is configured and which security policies apply to managed users and endpoints.

2. Purpose of the extension

The extension's single purpose is to protect enterprise users and organizational information by applying centrally managed browser security, data loss prevention (DLP), and AI-use policies.

Depending on organization policy, TruMotif Browser Guard may provide:

  • AI prompt inspection and policy enforcement;
  • sensitive-data and DLP detection;
  • file upload and download inspection;
  • local OCR-assisted inspection of supported images and documents;
  • Shadow AI service detection;
  • allowed and blocked security-event auditing;
  • central policy synchronization; and
  • managed endpoint and browser-profile association.

3. Information processed

The information processed depends on the policy configured by the deploying organization and on the user's activity. The extension may process the following categories:

Category Examples Purpose
Managed user / profile information Corporate browser-profile identity or email address when available through browser enterprise identity APIs. Associate policy and security events with the correct managed user or endpoint.
Prompt and page content Text submitted to supported AI services when inspection is enabled. DLP inspection, policy evaluation, allow/block/redact decisions, and security auditing.
File information and content Filename, type, size, extracted text, and content required to inspect supported uploads/downloads. Detect sensitive information and apply organization-configured file policies.
OCR-derived text Text extracted locally from supported image/document content. Evaluate content that cannot be inspected reliably as plain text.
Website / AI-service activity Domain, service, URL context, category, and risk metadata for configured AI services. Shadow AI discovery, service classification, policy enforcement, and security auditing.
Security and audit events Policy decision, event type, time, endpoint identifier, service, file metadata, and related security context. Enterprise monitoring, incident investigation, compliance evidence, and policy administration.
Technical information Extension version, browser information, endpoint identifier, policy version, synchronization state, and diagnostic errors. Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

TruMotif Browser Guard is not intended to collect information unrelated to its enterprise security purpose.

4. Local processing

Security inspection is designed to occur locally in the browser where practical. This includes policy evaluation and local processing used for supported file/text inspection and OCR workflows.

Local processing may temporarily use extension storage or browser-managed storage to hold policy state, processing state, or audit events awaiting delivery.

5. Information sent to the TruMotif control plane

TruMotif Browser Guard communicates with the organization's configured TruMotif control plane to obtain security policy and to deliver security/audit telemetry.

Depending on the organization's policy and the event involved, transmitted telemetry may include:

  • managed user or endpoint identifiers;
  • AI-service/domain information;
  • allow, block, redact, file, Shadow AI, and other security-event metadata;
  • policy decision and rule context;
  • file metadata and inspection results;
  • content or content-derived evidence when required by the organization's configured security policy; and
  • technical diagnostics required for reliable policy and audit synchronization.

The exact telemetry available to the organization depends on its deployment configuration and security policy.

6. How information is used

Information processed by the extension is used to:

  • detect and prevent unauthorized disclosure of organizational data;
  • apply browser, AI-use, DLP, and Shadow AI policies;
  • record allowed and blocked security activity;
  • support security operations and incident investigation;
  • maintain endpoint enrollment and policy synchronization;
  • diagnose reliability and security issues; and
  • provide the enterprise security functionality requested by the deploying organization.

Extension data is not used for behavioral advertising, advertising profiles, or consumer advertising personalization.

7. Sharing and sale of information

ParadigmIT Cybersecurity does not sell information processed by TruMotif Browser Guard for advertising or data-broker purposes.

Security information may be accessible to the organization that deployed TruMotif Browser Guard and to authorized service operators who need access to provide, secure, maintain, or support the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the service and its users.

8. Retention

Local extension state is retained only as needed for policy enforcement, reliable synchronization, and operation of the extension.

Retention of audit and security telemetry in the TruMotif control plane is determined by the applicable organization deployment, contractual terms, configured retention settings, and operational or legal requirements.

Requests concerning enterprise audit data should normally be directed to the organization that manages the user's device or TruMotif deployment.

9. Security

TruMotif Browser Guard uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect security data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to enterprise security data is restricted according to deployment and administrative controls.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Organizations are responsible for securely configuring and operating their TruMotif deployment, including control-plane access, endpoint enrollment, transport security, and administrative credentials.

10. User and organization choices

TruMotif Browser Guard is intended to be deployed and managed by organizations. Individual users may not be able to disable, remove, or change extension policies on an organization-managed browser.

Questions about monitoring, policy scope, retention, access, correction, or deletion of organization-controlled security records should be directed first to the organization managing the device or account.

11. Children

TruMotif Browser Guard is an enterprise security product and is not directed to children for personal or consumer use.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to TruMotif Browser Guard, legal requirements, or security practices. The date at the top of this page identifies the most recent revision.
